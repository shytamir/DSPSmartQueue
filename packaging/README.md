# DSP Smart Queue

Keep your next assembler from disappearing behind a parade of gears and circuit boards.

Smart Queue clears the clutter from your Replicator queue, showing the products you asked for while their intermediate parts craft behind the scenes. Queue an assembler, a Tesla tower, and some gears: those are the requests you see, in order.

- **Find your requests at a glance.** Icons and remaining quantities stay together.
- **Click what you mean.** Left-click selects the displayed recipe; right-click cancels that request.
- **Keep crafting as usual.** Crafting order, material returns, countdown, and progress follow the game's normal rules.

Gears you explicitly request still appear. Gears made along the way to an assembler stay out of sight. Everything fits into the familiar 14-slot strip.

## Installation

**Thunderstore Mod Manager:** Select Dyson Sphere Program and your profile, install DSP Smart Queue with its dependencies, then launch the game modded.

**Manual:** With BepInEx 5.4.17 installed and the game closed, extract `DSPSmartQueue.dll` from the download into `BepInEx/plugins/DSPSmartQueue/` inside your game folder. Create the Smart Queue folder if needed, then launch the game.

Open the Replicator and start crafting. There's nothing to configure.
